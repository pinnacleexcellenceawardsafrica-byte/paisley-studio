Overview

What is Key Stage 3?

Key Stage 3 covers Years 7 to 9 of the British National Curriculum, typically for children aged 11 to 14. It provides a broad and balanced education across a range of subjects, preparing pupils for the more specialised study of Key Stage 4.

  • Years 7, 8 and 9
  • Ages 11 to 14
  • Core subjects: English, Maths, Science
  • Foundation subjects: Computing, History, Geography, Languages, Art, Music, PE
  • National Curriculum for England
  • Preparation for IGCSE study
    • Subjects

    A wide range of subjects to explore.

    Key Stage 3 offers a broad curriculum covering all National Curriculum subjects.

    English

    Reading, writing, grammar, vocabulary, spoken English. Developing analytical and creative writing skills.

    Mathematics

    Number, algebra, ratio, proportion, geometry, statistics, probability. Developing mathematical reasoning and problem-solving.

    Science

    Biology, Chemistry, Physics. Developing scientific enquiry skills, critical thinking, and practical investigation.

    Computing

    Programming, algorithms, data representation, networks, online safety, digital literacy and creativity.

    History

    British history from 1066 to present day, world history, significant historical events, and historical enquiry skills.

    Geography

    Locational knowledge, place knowledge, human and physical geography, map skills, and fieldwork.

    Modern Languages

    French, Spanish, or other languages. Developing speaking, listening, reading and writing skills.

    Art & Design

    Drawing, painting, sculpture, printmaking, digital art. Exploring the work of artists and designers.

    Physical Education

    Games, gymnastics, dance, athletics, outdoor activities. Developing fitness, coordination, and teamwork.

    Learning Experience

    How your child will learn.

    Live Online Classes

    Interactive video lessons with qualified tutors. Small class sizes ensure every pupil gets attention.

    Assignments & Marking

    Regular homework and assignments marked by tutors with detailed feedback to support progress.

    Flexible Schedule

    Learning fits around your family's schedule. Lessons are recorded for revision and catch-up.
